# Evaluation method

`anomaly-detection-eval` replays synthetic series through the same model and pipeline code
that the sidecar runs, and scores the outcome on events, not on single samples.

## Data

Generated by `anomaly_detection.harness.synth`, reproducible from a seed.

| signal | shape |
|---|---|
| `request_rate` | base 120, daily harmonics (peak 15:00), weekend factor 0.65, +1 percent per week, 6 percent autocorrelated noise |
| `output_tokens` | base 280, +-5 percent daily, 3 percent noise |
| `throughput` | base 12000, +-2 percent daily, 1.5 percent noise, a 30 percent dip for two samples once an hour (checkpointing, normal) |
| `step_time` | derived: 96000 / throughput, plus 1 percent noise |

0.2 percent of samples are missing. Missing samples are skipped, never zero-filled.

Each case is 14 days of normal training data followed by an evaluation period (7 days for the
scenarios, 14 days for normal-only series). The models see the whole stream online, the way the
sidecar sees backfill and live data; only alerts in the evaluation period count.

## Scenarios

One anomaly of 60 to 240 minutes per scenario case, at a random time at least 12 hours into
the evaluation period. Even variants drop instantly, odd variants ramp over 5 minutes.

| scenario | signal, direction | injection |
|---|---|---|
| `request_drop` | `request_rate`, below | half of the variants fall to 0-5 percent of normal, half to 10-50 percent |
| `token_length_drop` | `output_tokens`, below | mean length becomes 1 to 3 tokens |
| `throughput_drop` | `throughput`, below | throughput falls to 45-55 percent |
| `step_time_rise` | `step_time`, above | the step time that follows from the same throughput drop |

Normal-only series use direction `both`, the worst case for false alarms, and are the source of
the false alarm rate per series per day.

## Alerts and metrics

- The alert rule is `score >= 0.5` for 3 consecutive samples (like `for: 3m` at a 60 second step).
  The alert fires at the third sample.
- Runs of high scores separated by at most 10 samples are one alert.
- A true event is detected when an alert fires from the event start until 5 samples after its end.
  Detection delay is the time from event start to that alert.
- Any alert that matches no event is a false alarm.
- Precision is matched alerts over all alerts, recall is detected events over events, F1 is the
  harmonic mean, false alarms per day is false alarms over evaluated days.
- An alert already firing at the start of the evaluation period counts as firing at its first
  sample.

## Comparison models

- `fixed`: lower limit at half of the minimum and upper limit at 1.5 times the maximum seen in
  the 14 training days. This is a generous fixed threshold: a tighter one would fire at the
  normal daily trough or peak.
- `seasonal`: defaults (daily and weekly seasonality, `band_k` 6, `slot_window` 8).
- `rcf`: defaults (shingle 8, 40 trees, sample 1024, 1440 sample normalisation window).
- `rcf-time`: `rcf` with time of day and week as extra dimensions.

## Tuning and final seeds

Parameters were tuned on seeds 0 to 19. The reported run uses seeds 1000 to 1019 for scenarios
and 1500 to 1504 for normal series, which were not used for tuning.

## What this does not show

- Synthetic data is simpler than production data. Results here say the models can do the job on
  a clean yardstick, not how many false alarms a real system produces.
- Resolution is one minute. The scenario delays are floor-limited by the `for` clause.
- Only single injected events, no overlapping failures, no gradual drift.
